01 — What is a Decision Tree?

A Decision Tree is a supervised machine learning algorithm used for classification and regression. It makes predictions by splitting data according to feature-based questions.

Think of it like a flowchart: answer one question, follow a branch, and continue until you reach a final prediction.

04 — How does it learn?

① Collect data

Gather examples with input features and known outcomes.

② Find a split

Choose a feature and threshold that help separate the target outcomes.

③ Grow branches

Repeat splitting on the resulting groups, subject to stopping rules.

④ Make a prediction

Use the reached leaf to predict a class or numeric value.

05 — Advantages & limitations

🌟 Advantages

• Easy to visualize and explain
• Can model nonlinear patterns
• Works with classification and regression
• Usually needs little feature scaling

🔍 Limitations

• Can overfit training data
• Small data changes may alter the tree
• A deep tree can become complex
• Pruning or depth limits may help

07 — Conclusion

Decision Trees are intuitive machine learning models that split data into smaller groups using feature-based rules. Their flowchart-like structure makes predictions easier to understand.

Remember: the quality of a tree depends on the data, the chosen splits, and how well the model generalizes to unseen examples.
